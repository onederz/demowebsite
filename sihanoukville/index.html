The Perfect Base in Sihanoukville

Onederz Sihanoukville is centrally located, just a 10-minute walk from the Tourism Pier. Close to Ochheuteal, Serendipity, and Otres beaches, as well as restaurants, bars, and transport options, it’s the ideal spot for both relaxation and exploring the city.

Explore Sihanoukville from Onederz

Stay in the heart of Sihanoukville with easy access to beaches, shopping, and dining. Enjoy the city’s coastal charm, relax by nearby beaches, or take a short drive to Otres for a quieter escape.

Ferry services from Tourism Pier make island hopping easy, including trips to Koh Rong and Koh Rong Samloem.

For adventure seekers, local markets, water sports, and boat tours are all within reach.

Getting to Onederz Sihanoukville

From Phnom Penh or Siem Reap

By Bus/Taxi: Travel to Sihanoukville (approx. 4–6 hours). Onederz is a short walk from the Tourism Pier.

From Nearby Islands

By Ferry: Daily ferries arrive at Tourism Pier, just 10 minutes from Onederz.
