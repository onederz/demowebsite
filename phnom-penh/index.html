Explore Phnom Penh from Our Doorstep

Step outside Onederz Phnom Penh and explore the lively riverside, bustling markets, and cultural landmarks. Our central location makes it easy to experience the city at your own pace.

Evenings come alive with bars, cafes, and night markets just minutes away. From delicious street food to cocktails with a view, Phnom Penh offers something for every traveler.

During the day, wander through historic streets, discover local crafts, or take a short tuk-tuk ride to iconic attractions like the Royal Palace and Wat Phnom.

Finding Your Way to Onederz

From Phnom Penh International Airport (PNH)

By Tuk-Tuk: Official stands available. Approx. $10–$12 USD, 30 minutes.

By Taxi: Comfortable for groups. Approx. $15–$20 USD.

From the Bus Station

By Tuk-Tuk: Most buses arrive at the city edge. Short tuk-tuk ride to Onederz, approx. $2–$4 USD.
